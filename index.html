Frequently Asked

What should I wear?

Indian formal / festive attire is warmly encouraged for all three events — think rich colours and comfortable footwear for dancing at the sangeet. Black is best avoided for the ceremony.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but to help us plan an evening for the adults, we've kept our celebrations 16-and-over except for immediate family.

Is there a wedding hashtag?

Yes — tag your photos #AnkitaMeetsSiddharth so we can find them all after the weekend.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence is truly the only present we need. For those who insist, details will be shared closer to the date.

Who do I contact with questions?

Reach out any time at ankita.siddharth.wedding@example.com — we're happy to help with travel, stay or anything in between.
